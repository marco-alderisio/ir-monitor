      <article data-history-node-id="13315" about="/data-breach-notifications" typeof="schema:WebPage">

  
      <span property="schema:name" content="Data Breach Notifications Directory" class="hidden"></span>


  
  <div>
    
            <div property="schema:text"><p>Data breach notices submitted to our office in accordance with&nbsp;<a href="https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=19.255">RCW 19.255</a> and&nbsp;<a href="https://apps.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=42.56.590">RCW 42.56.590</a> are published in the table below for public education purposes. To read a notice, click on the name of the organization in the list.</p>
<p>You can submit a data breach notice to our office online through our <a href="https://fortress.wa.gov/atg/formhandler/ago/databreachnotificationform.aspx">Data Breach Notification Web Form</a>. You can also find more information on submitting notices in the <a href="https://www.atg.wa.gov/data-breach-resources#businessesagencies">“Resources for Businesses and Agencies Affected by a Data Breach”</a> section of our Data Breach Resources page.</p>
<p>If you are a consumer who received a data breach notice, or you believe your personal information was lost or exposed and you want to know what steps to take to protect yourself, you can find more information in the <a>“</a><a href="https://www.atg.wa.gov/data-breach-resources#individuals">Resources for Individuals Affected by a Data Breach</a><a>” </a>section of our Data Breach Resources page.</p>
